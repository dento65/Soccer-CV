# Live demo script

1. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8000` and load the supplied sample.
2. Click **Find football events**. While it runs, say that CALF is pretrained and the project contribution begins once proposals arrive.
3. Click two timeline markers. Move the minimum-score control and show how accepted evidence changes.
4. Keep seconds before/after visible. Change from **Asymmetric context** to **Duration budget** and point to union duration and overlap removed.
5. Select one or more clip cards, click **Export MP4**, then use the completion dialog and Download MP4 link.
6. Return to the source, click **Track players and ball**, and show the tracking overlay. Explain green as a nearest-player proximity cue and red as a screen-position guide.
7. Point out that no ball detection means unknown evidence, not no possession or no event.

Fallback: a saved sample result can be loaded when CALF extraction is slow. Say it is a saved local model output, then demonstrate the policy controls and export live.
